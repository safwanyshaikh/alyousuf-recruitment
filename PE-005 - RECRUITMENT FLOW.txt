============================================================

Recruitment Flow is the operational backbone of KAI.

It governs the complete recruitment lifecycle from Client creation
to Client Submission.

It answers one question.

"How does manpower move from requirement to client submission?"

Recruitment Flow owns recruitment operations.

9. BUSINESS RULES
============================================================

BR-001

Every Requirement belongs to one Project.

BR-002

Every Project belongs to one Client.

BR-003

Campaign cannot exist without Requirement.

BR-004

Submission cannot exist without Campaign.

BR-005

Requirement remains source of truth.

BR-006

Multiple Campaigns allowed per Requirement.

BR-007

Multiple Submissions allowed per Campaign.

BR-008

History immutable.

BR-009

Closed Requirements remain searchable.

BR-010

Every operational movement creates Timeline.
