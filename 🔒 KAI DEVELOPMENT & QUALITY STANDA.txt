🔒 KAI DEVELOPMENT & QUALITY STANDARD

This is the final engineering standard that every developer, AI coding agent, and contributor must follow when building KAI.

It ensures that KAI remains one coherent operating system regardless of who writes the code.

---

# PURPOSE

Development inside KAI is not measured by

* Lines of Code
* Number of Features
* Sprint Velocity

Development is measured by

* Recruiter Time Saved
* Operational Simplicity
* Organizational Intelligence Created
* Architectural Consistency
* System Reliability

---

# DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY

Developers do not build screens.

Developers build operational capability.

Every commit should make recruitment

* Faster
* Simpler
* Safer
* More Intelligent
