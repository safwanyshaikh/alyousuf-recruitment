# Phase-1 Outcome

KAI Phase-1 establishes the complete recruitment operating system from:

**Client → Project → Requirement → Campaign → Submission → Candidate Intelligence → Sourcing Intelligence**

Mobilization, Visa, Deployment, and Executive Intelligence form Phase-2 and continue from the point where an offer is accepted.
