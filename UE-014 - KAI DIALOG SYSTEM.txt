RECRUITER RULES
============================================================

Dialogs never interrupt productivity.

Dialogs never ask unnecessary questions.

Dialogs always explain consequences.

Dialogs close immediately after completion.

============================================================
MANAGEMENT RULES
============================================================

Every dialog answers

What will happen?

Why?

Can it be reversed?

What happens next?

============================================================
LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Implement one universal Dialog component.

Never create module-specific dialogs.

Never use dialogs for large forms.

Prefer Intelligence Drawers for operational work.

Dialogs exist only for confirmation, safety and short interactions.
