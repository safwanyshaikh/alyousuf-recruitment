============================================================
LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Every incoming dataset must pass schema validation.

Raw data remains immutable.

Every ingested record receives metadata, lineage, Timeline and Audit records.

Duplicate operational records are prohibited.

Every ingestion pipeline must support retry, recovery and replay.
