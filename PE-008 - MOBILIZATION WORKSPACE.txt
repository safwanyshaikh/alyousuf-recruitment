============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

Mobilization Workspace governs the complete post-offer
pre-deployment lifecycle.

It begins only after the candidate accepts the offer and ends
when the candidate is ready for visa processing or travel,
depending on country workflow.

It answers one question.

"Is this candidate fully ready to mobilize?"
