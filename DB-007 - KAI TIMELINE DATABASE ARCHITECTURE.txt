LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Every successful business action must create exactly one immutable Timeline Event.

Timeline records are append-only.

Never modify historical events.

Every Timeline Event automatically links to Workflow, Audit,
Notifications, AI Intelligence and Analytics.

Timeline is the definitive chronological history of the KAI Operating System.
