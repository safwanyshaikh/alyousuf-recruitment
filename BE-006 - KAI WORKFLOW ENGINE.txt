============================================================
PURPOSE
============================================================

Create one universal Workflow Engine for the entire KAI
Workforce Intelligence Operating System.

Every operational activity must execute through the Workflow Engine.

No workflow logic may exist in controllers, repositories or UI.

============================================================
LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

No workflow logic outside the Workflow Engine.

Every business action executes through validated state transitions.

Every transition automatically updates Timeline, Queue,
Notifications, Audit and Analytics.

Workflow definitions must remain configurable without changing
business code where possible.
