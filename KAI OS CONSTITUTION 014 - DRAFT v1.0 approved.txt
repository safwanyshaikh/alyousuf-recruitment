# KAI OS CONSTITUTION 014

# MANAGEMENT CONSTITUTION

## Management Exists To Remove Operational Bottlenecks, Not Monitor People

**Status:** DRAFT v1.0

---

# PURPOSE

Management inside KAI does not supervise activity.

Management removes obstacles.

Managers are not expected to monitor recruiters.

Managers are expected to improve recruitment outcomes.

KAI therefore transforms management from supervision into operational leadership.

---

# MANAGEMENT PHILOSOPHY

Management should never ask

"What are my recruiters doing?"

Management should ask

"What is preventing recruitment from moving forward?"

KAI answers before the question is asked.

---

# MANAGEMENT HIERARCHY

Every management decision follows one hierarchy.

```text
Business Opportunity

↓

Operational Progress

↓

Operational Bottleneck

↓

Management Intervention

↓

Operational Recovery

↓

Business Outcome
```

No management screen shall violate this order.
