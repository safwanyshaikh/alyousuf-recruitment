Backend Engineering Specification v1.0

============================================================
PURPOSE
============================================================

Create one universal Domain Event Bus for the entire KAI
Workforce Intelligence Operating System.

Every successful business action must publish a Domain Event.

No module communicates directly with another module.

Everything communicates through Events.

============================================================
LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Every successful business transaction publishes one or more
Domain Events.

Subscribers must never directly depend on each other.

Timeline, Queue, Notification, Audit, Reporting and AI react only
through Domain Events.

Never invoke downstream modules directly after business execution.

The Event Bus is the single communication backbone of KAI.
