LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Every scheduled job must execute through the centralized Scheduler Engine.

Every automation trigger must be uniquely identifiable and idempotent.

Every scheduled execution must support retry, recovery and auditability.

Every scheduler execution automatically generates Timeline and Audit records.

No production automation may execute through unmanaged schedulers or external timing mechanisms.
