### Client Submission Register

KAI does not eliminate client submission sheets.

Instead, KAI automatically generates them.

Many overseas clients request candidates in:

* Excel
* Client Template
* Submission Sheet
* Company Format

Recruiters normally submit candidates in batches.

Examples:

* Batch 1 of 5 Candidates
* Batch 2 of 8 Candidates
* Batch 3 of 10 Candidates

Every submission batch becomes a permanent Submission Register inside KAI.

If the client has **no prescribed format**, KAI automatically generates the default submission sheet.

### Default KAI Submission Format

| Sr. No | Ref No (KAI No) | Candidate Name | Nationality | Highest Qualification | Total Experience | Position Applied | Gulf Experience | Salary Expectation | Recruiter Remarks | Client Remarks |
| ------ | --------------- | -------------- | ----------- | --------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- | --------------- | ------------------ | ----------------- | -------------- |

**Ref No** shall always be the KAI Number.

The recruiter only selects candidates.

KAI automatically populates all available information from the candidate profile.

If a client provides its own submission template, KAI maps the required fields and auto-populates the client format, minimizing manual typing.

Every submission batch is permanently stored inside the Submission Workspace with:

* Batch Number
* Submission Date & Time
* Requirement
* Client
* Recruiter
* Candidate List
* Client Submission Format Used
* Submission Version History
* Client Feedback
* Interview Outcome

No recruiter should recreate the same Excel twice.

KAI generates every submission register automatically while maintaining complete submission history for future reference, replacements, audits, and repeat projects.
