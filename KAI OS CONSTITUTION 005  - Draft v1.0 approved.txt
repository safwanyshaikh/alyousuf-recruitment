# KAI OS CONSTITUTION 005

# INTERACTION CONSTITUTION

## Every Interaction Must Reduce Operational Effort

**Status:** DRAFT v1.0

---

# PURPOSE

Interaction is the bridge between recruiter thinking and operational execution.

KAI shall never force recruiters to learn software.

KAI shall behave the way experienced recruiters naturally work.

Every interaction shall reduce effort.

Every interaction shall reduce interruption.

Every interaction shall reduce context switching.

---

# INTERACTION PHILOSOPHY

The recruiter should never ask

"Where do I click?"

The recruiter should ask only

"What should I do next?"

KAI is responsible for making the answer obvious.

---

# ARTICLE X001

## One Continuous Workspace

Recruitment is continuous.

The interface shall therefore feel continuous.

Changing screens shall be exceptional.

Working inside the same workspace shall be the default behaviour.

---

# ARTICLE X002

## Context Never Breaks

Changing

Candidate

Requirement

Campaign

Submission

Partner

Every interaction preserves

Search

Filters

Selection

Queue Position

Scroll Position

Timeline

Notes

Working Memory

# ARTICLE X004

## One-Hand Rule

Mouse

or

editing occurs inline.

Priority

Remark

Next Follow-up

# ARTICLE X006

Bulk Assignment

Bulk Remarks

Bulk Communication

Bulk Export

Bulk Submission

Bulk Follow-up

Bulk Ownership

---

# ARTICLE X007

## Search Is Always Available

Search is permanent.

Search never disappears.

Search never changes location.

Every recruiter develops muscle memory.

---

# ARTICLE X008

## Zero Navigation Anxiety

Where they are.

Why they are here.

What happened previously.

What happens next.

# INTERACTION VALIDATION

Every future interaction must answer

1.

Does this reduce recruiter effort?

2.

Does this reduce context switching?

3.

Can this happen without opening another screen?

4.

Can this become an inline action?

5.

Can this become a bulk action?

6.

Will this feel natural to an experienced GCC recruiter?

7.

Does this remove operational friction?

If any answer is NO,

the interaction shall be redesigned.

---

The operating system should feel invisible.

Recruiters should feel they are recruiting,

not operating software.

↓

Interaction Constitution
