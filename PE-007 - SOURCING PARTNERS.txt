============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

Sourcing Partners is the permanent sourcing intelligence network
of the agency.

It manages every external manpower source that supports the agency.

It is an internal strategic asset.

It is never exposed to clients or the public.

It preserves relationships, performance and sourcing capability
across years.
