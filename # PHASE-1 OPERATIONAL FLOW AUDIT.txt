# Responsibility of Each Layer

## KAI Administration

Creates and governs agencies.

Output

Agency becomes operational.

↓

## Tenant Administration

Configures the agency.

Creates recruiters.

Assigns permissions.

Creates master data.

Assigns work.

Output

Recruiters receive operational access.

↓

## Recruiters & Roles

Authentication layer.

Determines user role.

Output

Routes user to the correct dashboard.

↓

## Management Dashboard

Purpose

Decision making.

Questions answered

* Which requirement is critical?
* Which campaign needs intervention?
* Which recruiter needs support?
* Which client is waiting?
* Which project is delayed?
* Which sourcing partner should receive more work?
* Which submission requires escalation?

Management never recruits from this screen.

It directs operations.

↓

## Recruiter Dashboard

Purpose

Execution.

Questions answered

* What do I work on first?
* Which candidate do I call?
* Which submission is pending?
* Which interview is today?
* Which campaign needs sourcing?
* Which requirement needs manpower?

Recruiters execute from here.

↓

## Clients

Business relationship.

↓

## Projects

Commercial scope.

↓

## Requirements

Manpower demand.

↓

## Campaigns

Sourcing execution.

↓

## Candidates

Internal workforce intelligence.

↓

## Sourcing Partners

External workforce intelligence.

Both continuously feed manpower into the same Requirement.

Neither owns the candidate.

↓

## Submission Workspace

Operational desk.

Creates submission batches.

Tracks client responses.

Tracks interviews.

Tracks offers.

Tracks replacements.

↓

## Client Submission

Client receives the submission batch.

Client reviews.

Client shortlists.

Client interviews.

Client selects.

Offer released.

Phase-1 ends here.

Mobilization begins in Phase-2.

# Phase-1 End Boundary

Phase-1 officially ends when the client has received the submission and completed the recruitment decision (shortlist, interview, selection, and offer release).

Once the candidate accepts the offer, ownership transfers to the Mobilization Workspace, which is the beginning of Phase-2.
