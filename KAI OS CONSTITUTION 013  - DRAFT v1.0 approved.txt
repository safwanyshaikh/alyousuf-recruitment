# KAI OS CONSTITUTION 013

# DASHBOARD CONSTITUTION

## Dashboards Exist To Direct Decisions, Never To Display Reports

**Status:** DRAFT v1.0

---

# PURPOSE

Dashboards are the front door of KAI.

They do not exist to impress users.

They do not exist to display statistics.

They exist to answer one question immediately.

**"What requires my attention right now?"**

If a dashboard cannot answer that question within 30 seconds,

it has failed.

---

# DASHBOARD PHILOSOPHY

Dashboards never become reporting systems.

Dashboards become operational command centres.

Every card,

every widget,

every alert,

every graph,

must immediately lead to operational work.

Observation without action has no value.

---

# DASHBOARD HIERARCHY

Every dashboard shall follow one universal hierarchy.

```text
Critical Alerts

↓

Today's Priorities

↓

Operational Queues

↓

Bottlenecks

↓

Upcoming Commitments

↓

Performance Snapshot

↓

Recent Activity

↓

Executive Trends
```

No dashboard may violate this order.

Agency Operations

# ARTICLE DB002

## Dashboards Never Replace Workspaces

Dashboards answer

"What needs attention?"

Workspaces answer

"Complete the work."

Dashboards launch work.

Workspaces complete work.

---

# DASHBOARD VALIDATION

Every future dashboard must answer

1.

Can users understand today's priorities within 30 seconds?

2.

Does every visible element support an operational decision?

3.

Does every widget lead directly to work?

4.

Are bottlenecks immediately visible?

5.

Would removing this widget make operations harder?

6.

Does the dashboard reduce meetings?

7.

Does the dashboard reduce manual reporting?

If any answer is NO,

the dashboard shall be redesigned.
