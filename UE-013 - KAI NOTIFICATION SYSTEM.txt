RECRUITER RULES
============================================================

Never receive duplicate notifications.

Never receive passive notifications.

Every notification must be actionable.

Every notification must disappear after resolution.

============================================================
MANAGEMENT RULES
============================================================

Notifications answer

What happened?

Why?

Who must act?

When?

What happens next?

============================================================
LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Implement one universal Notification Engine.

Never create module-specific notifications.

Always group intelligently.

Always prioritize action over information.

Every notification must connect directly to Workspace, Drawer, Queue and Timeline.
