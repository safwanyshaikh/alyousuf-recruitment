============================================================
8. BUSINESS RULES
============================================================

BR-001

Agency cannot create another Agency.

BR-002

Agency cannot modify Platform configuration.

BR-003

Agency owns only its users.

BR-004

Recruiter belongs to one Agency.

BR-005

Recruiter may belong to multiple Departments.

BR-006

Agency Admin cannot access another Agency.

BR-007

Historical ownership never changes.

BR-008

Suspended users retain audit history.

BR-009

Deleted users become Archived.

BR-010

Platform permissions override Agency permissions.
