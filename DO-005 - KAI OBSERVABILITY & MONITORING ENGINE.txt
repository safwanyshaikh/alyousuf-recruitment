LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Every production component must expose metrics, logs and traces.

Every request must carry Correlation ID and Trace ID.

Every critical failure must automatically generate Timeline, Audit and Alert records.

Monitoring failures must never interrupt production services.

Every dashboard displays real-time operational health.
