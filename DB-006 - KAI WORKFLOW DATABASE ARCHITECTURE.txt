============================================================
LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Every operational entity must execute through a Workflow Instance.

Never update business state without recording a Workflow Transition.

Every transition automatically generates Timeline, Audit,
Metrics, AI Intelligence and History.

Workflow history is immutable and replayable.
