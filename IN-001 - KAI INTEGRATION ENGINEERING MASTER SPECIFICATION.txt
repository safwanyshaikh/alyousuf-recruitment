LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Every integration must pass through the Integration Layer.

No external system directly accesses operational databases.

Every request and response must generate Timeline and Audit records.

Every connector must support retries, monitoring, versioning and rollback.

Every integration failure must be isolated without affecting core operations.
