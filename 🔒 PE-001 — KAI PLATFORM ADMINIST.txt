# PURPOSE

To govern the KAI platform.

KAI Administration manages the platform itself.

It never participates in recruitment.

It creates and governs tenant agencies.

---

# GOLDEN QUESTION

# BUSINESS RULES

BR-001

Platform creates Agencies.

---

BR-002

Agencies never create themselves.

---

BR-003

Tenant isolation mandatory.

---

BR-004

Agency cannot access another Agency.

---

BR-005

Platform controls licensing.

---

BR-006

Agency controls recruiters.

---

BR-007

Platform never edits recruitment data.

---

BR-008

Agency deletion never deletes historical audit.

---

BR-009

Every Agency owns independent storage.

---

BR-010

Platform AI never exposes tenant data.
