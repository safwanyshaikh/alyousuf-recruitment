1. PURPOSE
============================================================

Deployment Workspace governs the complete lifecycle from Travel
Ready until Candidate joins the employer and becomes Alumni.

It permanently records deployment history and converts every
deployment into organizational intelligence.

It answers one question.

"Has the candidate successfully joined and what can be learned?"
