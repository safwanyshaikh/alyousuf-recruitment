============================================================
LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Every operation exceeding 500ms must execute as a background job.

Never block API responses while processing.

Every background job must publish Domain Events.

Every job automatically updates Timeline, Queue, Notifications,
Audit and Monitoring.

Workers must remain stateless, idempotent and horizontally scalable.
