Administration Module — Operational Constitution v1.0

---

# Purpose

Administration is not a CRM.

Administration is not Recruitment.

Administration is the control room of one recruitment agency inside KAI OS.

Its responsibility is only to configure, secure, and manage the agency.

Recruiters should almost never need to open this module.

Everything here exists to support recruiters, not slow them down.

---

# Multi-Tenant Principle

KAI is a multi-agency operating system.

Every recruitment agency operates inside its own secure environment.

Example

KAI Platform

│

├── AL Yousuf Enterprises LLP

├── Jai HR Consultants

├── ABC Overseas

├── XYZ Recruitment

Each agency has complete control only over its own organization.

No agency can access another agency.

KAI Super Admin remains completely independent.

# Golden Rule

Administration answers only one question.

> **Who is allowed to access what inside this agency?**

It never becomes part of the recruitment workflow.

---

# Success Criteria

An Agency Admin should be able to configure, secure, manage, monitor, and operate the entire recruitment organization—including users, permissions, master data, recruiter assignments, productivity, security, and organizational settings—without requiring KAI Platform intervention.

At the same time, recruiters should spend their entire working day inside Recruitment Flow, Candidates, Sourcing Partners, and Submission Workspace without ever needing to open Administration.

Administration remains invisible during recruitment and powerful behind the scenes.
