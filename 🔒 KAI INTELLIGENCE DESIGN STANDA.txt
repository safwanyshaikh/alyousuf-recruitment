# 🔒 KAI INTELLIGENCE DESIGN STANDARD

This is the document that transforms KAI from software into a Workforce Intelligence Operating System.

Every intelligence displayed anywhere in KAI must follow this standard.

---

# Purpose

Recruiters should never ask

> "What should I do?"

KAI should always answer

* What happened?
* Why?
* What should happen next?
* How confident am I?
* What happens if I don't act?

# Intelligence Validation

Every intelligence must answer

* Is it useful?
* Is it explainable?
* Is it actionable?
* Is it evidence based?
* Does it reduce recruiter thinking?
* Does it improve organizational knowledge?
* Can the recruiter ignore it safely?

If not,

remove it.

---

# Intelligence Golden Rule

KAI never displays intelligence for decoration.

Every intelligence block must immediately improve a recruitment decision.
