1. PURPOSE

This Constitution establishes the permanent standards governing
coordination, orchestration, delegation and execution between
multiple KAI Agents operating within the KAI Workforce
Intelligence Operating System.

Every workflow shall have one orchestrator.

Every participating Agent shall have one defined responsibility.

Every orchestration shall remain deterministic, observable and
fully auditable.
