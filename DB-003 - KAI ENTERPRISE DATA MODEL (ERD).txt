============================================================
LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Candidate is the permanent master entity.

Every business object must relate back to Candidate, Client,
Project or Organization.

Never duplicate entities.

Use bridge tables for all many-to-many relationships.

Every entity automatically supports Timeline, Audit,
Versioning, Tenant Isolation and Soft Delete.
