# KAI OS CONSTITUTION 019

# GOVERNANCE CONSTITUTION

## Governance Protects Trust, Identity, and Organisational Intelligence

**Status:** DRAFT v1.0

---

# PURPOSE

Governance exists to protect the integrity of KAI.

Every record,

every decision,

every workflow,

every intelligence,

every deployment

must remain trustworthy.

Without trust,

there is no Workforce Intelligence.

---

# GOVERNANCE PHILOSOPHY

Everything inside KAI must answer one question.

**Can this be trusted five years from now?**

If the answer is uncertain,

the process shall be redesigned.
