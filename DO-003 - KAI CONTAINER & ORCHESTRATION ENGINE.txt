PURPOSE

Create one universal Container & Orchestration Engine for the

LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Every production service must execute inside immutable containers.

Every container image must be scanned and signed before deployment.

Every workload must support self-healing and horizontal scaling.

No container may run with unnecessary privileges.

Every deployment generates Timeline and Audit records.
