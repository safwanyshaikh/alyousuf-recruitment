============================================================
LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Every enterprise master entity must have exactly one Golden Record.

Duplicate master records are prohibited.

Every master data modification requires versioning and governance.

Every master data operation automatically generates Metadata, Lineage, Timeline and Audit records.

All downstream systems consume master data from the MDM Engine only.
