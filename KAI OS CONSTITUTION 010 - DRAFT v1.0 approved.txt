# KAI OS CONSTITUTION 010

# QUEUE CONSTITUTION

## Work Flows Through Queues. Never Through Lists.

**Status:** DRAFT v1.0

---

# PURPOSE

Recruiters do not manage records.

Recruiters manage work.

Lists become databases.

Queues become operations.

KAI therefore replaces static lists with operational queues.

Every operational object inside KAI shall belong to an active queue.

Nothing waits without visibility.

Nothing moves without ownership.

---

# QUEUE PHILOSOPHY

Recruiters never ask

"Which record should I open?"

Recruiters ask

"What should I do next?"

The queue answers.

---

# QUEUE HIERARCHY

Every operational object follows one universal queue lifecycle.

```text
Incoming

↓

Assigned

↓

Working

↓

Waiting

↓

Blocked

↓

Completed

↓

Archived
```

Every module inherits this lifecycle.

---

# ARTICLE Q001

## Queues Own Work

Lists store records.

Queues own operational responsibility.

Candidates,

Requirements,

Campaigns,

Submissions,

Partners,

Visa Cases,

Deployments

must always belong to an operational queue.

---

# ARTICLE Q002

## Queue Before Status

Status describes.

Queue prioritises.

Example

Status

Interview Scheduled

Queue

Waiting For Client Interview

Recruiters work from queues.

Not statuses.

---

# ARTICLE Q003

## Every Queue Has An Owner

No queue exists without ownership.

Ownership belongs to

Recruiter

Team

Department

Automation

Management

or

System.

Unowned work is operational risk.

---

# ARTICLE Q004

## Queue Priority

Every queue automatically orders work by

Critical

Urgent

High

Normal

Low

Priority changes automatically with operational reality.

Never manually sort work every morning.

---

# ARTICLE Q005

## Incoming Queue

New operational work enters only through Incoming.

Examples

New Candidate

New Requirement

New Partner

New Campaign

New Submission Request

Incoming is triage.

Not execution.

---

# ARTICLE Q006

## Assigned Queue

Assigned work belongs to one owner.

Assignment immediately creates accountability.

Nothing remains "floating."

---

# ARTICLE Q007

## Working Queue

Working Queue contains active execution.

Calling

Screening

Updating

Interview Coordination

Submission

Communication

Everything currently in progress belongs here.

---

# ARTICLE Q008

## Waiting Queue

Waiting Queue contains work dependent on external response.

Examples

Candidate Reply

Client Feedback

Offer Acceptance

Visa Approval

Document Submission

Medical

Recruiters should not repeatedly search for waiting work.

---

# ARTICLE Q009

## Blocked Queue

Blocked Queue contains work that cannot progress.

Every blocked item must display

Reason

Owner

Dependency

Expected Resolution

Blocked work is immediately visible to management.

---

# ARTICLE Q010

## Completed Queue

Completed Queue confirms operational completion.

Completed work remains searchable.

It immediately strengthens organisational intelligence.

---

# ARTICLE Q011

## Archived Queue

Archive never means deleted.

Archive means

Operationally complete

Historically valuable

Every archived object remains part of Workforce Intelligence.

---

# ARTICLE Q012

## Queue Movement Is Automatic

Whenever possible,

KAI moves work automatically.

Example

Interview Scheduled

↓

Waiting Queue

Interview Completed

↓

Working Queue

Offer Signed

↓

Mobilization Queue

Recruiters should not spend time moving records manually.
