# KAI OS CONSTITUTION 003

# DECISION CONSTITUTION

## Every Screen Exists To Make One Decision

**Status:** DRAFT v1.0

---

# PURPOSE

Software should never become another decision.

Software should reduce decisions.

Every screen inside KAI exists to help the user make exactly one operational decision.

If a screen requires users to think about the software itself,

the screen has failed.

---

# DECISION HIERARCHY

Every decision belongs to one of three levels.

```text
Strategic Decision

↓

Operational Decision

↓

Execution Decision
```

No screen shall mix all three.

---

# ARTICLE D001

## One Screen. One Decision.

Every workspace answers one primary operational question.

Nothing else.

Secondary information exists only to support that decision.

---

# ARTICLE D002

## KAI Administration

Decision

Which agencies may operate inside KAI?

Output

Agency created

Agency suspended

Agency licensed

Agency activated

---

# ARTICLE D003

## Tenant Administration

Decision

Who should have access to what?

Output

User

Permission

Assignment

Security

Capacity

---

# ARTICLE D004

## Management Dashboard

Decision

Where should management intervene today?

Never answer

Who worked harder?

Never become an attendance system.

Never become employee surveillance.

Management Dashboard exists only to remove operational bottlenecks.

---

# ARTICLE D005

## Recruiter Dashboard

Decision

What should I work on now?

Every widget must reduce uncertainty.

The recruiter should begin working within seconds.

---

# ARTICLE D006

## Clients

Decision

Should this business opportunity become an active recruitment project?

Output

Project

Not recruitment.

---

# ARTICLE D007

## Projects

Decision

Is this commercial opportunity ready for manpower execution?

Output

Requirements.

---

# ARTICLE D008

## Requirements

Decision

What manpower must be delivered?

Everything inside Requirements exists to answer

Trade

Quantity

Salary

Timeline

Priority

Client Expectations

Nothing more.

---

# ARTICLE D009

## Campaigns

Decision

Where can the required manpower be sourced fastest with acceptable quality?

Campaign never measures recruiter activity.

Campaign measures manpower availability.

---

# ARTICLE D010

## Candidates

Decision

Is this person suitable for the current opportunity?

The screen must answer

Availability

Suitability

Readiness

Risk

Next Action

Recruiters should never assemble this mentally.

---

# ARTICLE D011

## Contact Dictionary

Decision

Should this contact remain a contact,

or become a candidate?

The system shall never force profile creation before sufficient information exists.

---

# ARTICLE D012

## Sourcing Partners

Decision

Which sourcing partner should receive this requirement?

Decision based upon

Speed

Quality

Reliability

Trade

Location

Historical Performance

Relationship

Not personal preference.

## Visa

---

# ARTICLE D016

## Deployment

Has recruitment successfully become employment?

---

# ARTICLE D017

## Dashboards Never Execute Work

Dashboards answer questions.

Workspaces execute work.

This separation is mandatory.

---

# ARTICLE D018

## Decision Before Data

Every screen shall always display

Decision

↓

Recommendation

↓

Evidence

↓

History

↓

Raw Data

Never reverse this order.

---

# ARTICLE D019

## Every Decision Produces Work

A decision without action has no operational value.

Every approved decision must immediately create

Assignment

Queue

Reminder

Timeline

Ownership

or Workflow Progress.

---

# ARTICLE D020

## No Duplicate Decisions

The same operational decision shall never be requested twice.

Example

Once suitability has been approved,

future screens consume that decision.

They do not ask the recruiter again.

---

# ARTICLE D021

## Explain Before Approval

Whenever KAI recommends something,

it shall explain

Why

Evidence

Confidence

Risk

Human approval remains mandatory.

---

# ARTICLE D022

## Escalation

If no operational decision can be made safely,

KAI escalates.

It never guesses.

Escalation becomes visible to the responsible owner.

---

# DECISION VALIDATION

Every future screen must pass.

1.

What single decision does this screen exist to support?

2.

Who owns that decision?

3.

What information is essential?

4.

What information is distracting?

5.

What action follows immediately after the decision?

If these cannot be answered,

the screen shall not be implemented.

---

# LOCK CONDITION

Every module,

dashboard,

drawer,

dialog,

popup,

table,

assistant,

and automation inside KAI shall exist to improve decision quality.

The Decision Constitution derives from

Human Constitution

↓

Operational Constitution

↓

Decision Constitution

No future implementation may violate this order.
