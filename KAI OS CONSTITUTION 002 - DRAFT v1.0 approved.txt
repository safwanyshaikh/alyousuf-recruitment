# KAI OS CONSTITUTION 002

# OPERATIONAL CONSTITUTION

## The Movement of Work

**Status:** DRAFT v1.0

---

# PURPOSE

The Operational Constitution defines how recruitment work flows through KAI.

It establishes ownership.

It establishes responsibility.

It establishes handover.

It establishes continuity.

Every operational activity inside KAI must belong to exactly one module.

No work may exist without ownership.

No ownership may exist twice.

---

The recruitment lifecycle shall always follow one immutable operational chain.

This chain may never be reordered.

---

# ARTICLE O001

## Every Module Owns One Responsibility

Each module owns one operational responsibility.

When that responsibility is completed,

ownership transfers to the next module.

No module may perform the responsibility of another.

---

# ARTICLE O002

## Agency Owns Operations

Agency owns

People

Processes

Policies

Recruiters

Permissions

Clients

Commercial Operations

Agency never owns candidates.

Candidates belong to the Workforce Intelligence Database.

---

# ARTICLE O003

## Client Owns Business

Client represents commercial ownership.

Client answers only one question.

Who awarded this work?

Client never owns manpower.

Client never owns sourcing.

Client never owns submissions.

---

# ARTICLE O004

## Project Owns Commercial Execution

Projects organise recruitment commercially.

Every Requirement belongs to one Project.

Projects never own Candidates.

Projects never own Campaigns.

Projects organise work.

---

# ARTICLE O005

## Requirement Owns Demand

Requirement owns manpower demand.

Everything downstream exists because of the Requirement.

If a Requirement closes,

Campaigns close.

Submission closes.

Candidate ownership returns to Workforce Intelligence.

---

# ARTICLE O006

## Campaign Owns Sourcing

Campaign exists only to discover manpower.

Campaign may source through

Database

Contact Dictionary

Sourcing Partners

Training Centres

Education Institutes

Advertisements

Walk-ins

Referrals

Campaign never owns candidates.

Campaign contributes candidates.

---

# ARTICLE O007

## Candidate Supply Owns Identity

Every human being exists only once.

Candidates remain permanent.

Requirements change.

Projects change.

Campaigns change.

Clients change.

Candidate identity never changes.

---

# ARTICLE O008

## Submission Owns Client Delivery

Submission begins after sourcing.

Submission owns

Internal Shortlisting

Submission Batches

Client Submission

Client Feedback

Interview Coordination

Offer Tracking

Replacement Tracking

Submission ends when Offer is accepted.

---

# ARTICLE O009

## Mobilization Owns Travel Readiness

Mobilization begins after Offer Acceptance.

Recruitment ownership ends.

Operational deployment ownership begins.

---

# ARTICLE O010

## Visa Owns Government Compliance

Visa Workspace owns

Documentation

Medical

Embassy

Consulate

MOFA

eMigrate

Immigration

Recruiters no longer own these activities.

---

# ARTICLE O011

## Deployment Owns Completion

Deployment begins when candidate joins employer.

Deployment records

Joining

Arrival

Completion

Replacement

Return

Deployment closes operational recruitment.

---

# ARTICLE O012

## Alumni Never Leave KAI

Deployment does not end the relationship.

Candidate becomes Alumni.

Alumni become Future Opportunities.

Every completed deployment strengthens Workforce Intelligence.

---

# ARTICLE O013

## No Operational Duplication

No Candidate

No Requirement

No Campaign

No Submission

No Partner

No Client

may exist twice.

Duplicates weaken intelligence.

Duplicates are operational failures.

# ARTICLE O016

## Recruitment Never Starts Twice

A Requirement starts once.

Campaigns expand it.

Submissions fulfil it.

Replacement continues it.

KAI never recreates work that already exists.

# OPERATIONAL VALIDATION

Every new feature must answer

1.

Which module owns this work?

2.

Which module receives ownership next?

3.

Does this duplicate another module?

4.

Does this strengthen Workforce Intelligence?

5.

Can ownership become ambiguous?

If ambiguity exists,

the feature shall not enter KAI.

---

# LOCK CONDITION

The Operational Constitution governs every workflow inside KAI.

No module,

screen,

automation,

AI,

dashboard,

or future feature

may violate this operational chain.

The Operational Constitution derives from the Human Constitution and becomes the permanent workflow backbone of KAI OS.
