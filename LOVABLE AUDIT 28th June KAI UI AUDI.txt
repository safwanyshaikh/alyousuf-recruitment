# Final Contact View Audit

The Contact View is confirmed as the primary Human Intelligence Workspace for recruiters.

Its architecture is strong and recruiter-centric.

The remaining work is not structural replacement. It is the controlled surfacing of K14 intelligence, deployment context, and recruiter guidance using the intelligence already generated by the backend, so that the interface consistently answers:

* What is this candidate's current state?
* What is the best deployment opportunity?
* Why is that recommendation being made?
* What should the recruiter do next?
