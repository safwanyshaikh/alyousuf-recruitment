# KAI OS CONSTITUTION 016

# ADMINISTRATION CONSTITUTION

## Administration Exists To Enable Operations, Never To Control Them

**Status:** DRAFT v1.0

---

# PURPOSE

Administration governs the operating environment.

It does not perform recruitment.

It does not interfere with recruitment.

It establishes

Identity

Permissions

Security

Configuration

Governance

Every operational activity inside KAI depends upon Administration,

yet Administration shall remain almost invisible during daily recruitment.

---

# ADMINISTRATION PHILOSOPHY

Administration creates the environment.

Recruiters create recruitment.

Management creates direction.

KAI creates intelligence.

Responsibilities never overlap.

---

# ADMINISTRATION HIERARCHY

Administration follows one governance hierarchy.

```text
KAI Platform

↓

Tenant Agency

↓

Agency Administrator

↓

Departments

↓

Recruiters

↓

Operational Ownership
```

No level may bypass another without platform authorization.

---

# ARTICLE A001

## Two Independent Administrations

KAI contains two completely separate administration layers.

### Platform Administration (KAI)

Owned exclusively by KAI.

### Tenant Administration (Agency)

Owned exclusively by the licensed agency.

Neither administration shall interfere with the other.

---

# ARTICLE A002

## Platform Administration (KAI)

Only KAI Platform Administration may

Create Agencies

Activate Agencies

Suspend Agencies

Delete Agencies

Assign Subscription Plans

Allocate Recruiter Capacity

Manage Platform Intelligence

Manage Global AI Models

Platform Configuration

Security Policies

Product Features

Platform administrators never participate in recruitment operations.

---

# ARTICLE A003

## Tenant Administration

Each licensed agency receives one Tenant Administrator.

The Tenant Administrator governs only that agency.

Never another agency.

Examples

Al Yousuf Enterprise LLP

↓

Agency Administrator

↓

Recruiters

↓

Recruitment Operations

Agency isolation is absolute.

---

Each tenant owns

Agency Name

Logo

Users

Departments

Partner Network

Operational Data

KAI owns the platform.

The agency owns its operations.

# ARTICLE A005

## Recruiter Provisioning

Recruiters

Permissions

not days.

---

# ARTICLE A006

## Permission Philosophy

Permissions are responsibility based.

Never title based.

Permissions exist only to support operational ownership.

Users receive the minimum permissions required to perform their work.

---

# ARTICLE A007

## Operational Isolation

Every tenant operates independently.

No recruiter,

manager,

administrator,

or AI

shall access another tenant's operational information.

Agency boundaries are permanent.

---

Departments

Defaults

without changing KAI architecture.

KAI remains one operating system.

Not multiple customised products.

---

# ADMINISTRATION VALIDATION

Every future administrative feature must answer

1.

Does this strengthen governance?

2.

Does this preserve agency isolation?

3.

Does this improve operational continuity?

4.

Does this reduce administrative complexity?

5.

Does this protect organisational knowledge?

6.

Does this avoid interfering with recruitment?

7.

Can this scale across thousands of agencies?

If any answer is NO,

the feature shall be redesigned.
