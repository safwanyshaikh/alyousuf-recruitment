1. PURPOSE
============================================================

Visa Workspace governs the complete immigration and employer
approval lifecycle after Mobilization.

It begins when Mobilization is completed and ends when the
candidate becomes Travel Ready.

It answers one question.

"Can this candidate legally travel and join the employer?"
