LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Every service-to-service request must traverse the Service Mesh.

All internal communication must use mutual TLS.

Every service interaction automatically generates Trace, Timeline and Audit records.

Routing policies must remain centralized and independently deployable.

No application implements communication logic already provided by the Service Mesh.
