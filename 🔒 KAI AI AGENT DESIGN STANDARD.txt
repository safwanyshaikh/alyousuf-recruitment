🔒 KAI AI AGENT DESIGN STANDARD

This document governs every AI agent inside KAI.

Not chatbots.

Not assistants.

**Operational AI Workforce.**

Each AI Agent owns one responsibility.

Never overlaps.

Never competes.

Never replaces recruiters.

---

# PURPOSE

AI inside KAI exists to eliminate thinking effort,

not human judgement.

Every AI Agent should continuously work in the background,

making recruiters feel they have an experienced recruitment coordinator working beside them.

---

# AI PHILOSOPHY

AI never becomes the recruiter.

AI becomes

Research Assistant

Recruitment Coordinator

Compliance Checker

Relationship Memory

Risk Advisor

Submission Assistant

Knowledge Engine

Recruiter always owns the final decision.
