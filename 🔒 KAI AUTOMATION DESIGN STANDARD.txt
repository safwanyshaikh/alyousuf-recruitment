🔒 KAI AUTOMATION DESIGN STANDARD

This document governs every automation inside KAI.

Not technical automation.

**Operational automation.**

Automation exists to eliminate repetitive recruiter work while preserving human control.

---

# PURPOSE

Recruiters should never spend time on work that software can safely perform.

KAI automates

Remembering

Tracking

Monitoring

Following

Calculating

Validating

Linking

Not

Recruitment judgement.
