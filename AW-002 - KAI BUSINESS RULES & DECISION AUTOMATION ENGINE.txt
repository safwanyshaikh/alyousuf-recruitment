LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Every enterprise decision must execute through the centralized Rule Engine.

Every business rule must have one approved owner and one active production version.

Every rule modification must remain version controlled and reversible.

Every rule execution automatically generates Timeline and Audit records.

No enterprise workflow may contain unmanaged or duplicated business rules.
