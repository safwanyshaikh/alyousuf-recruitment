============================================================
LOVABLE RULES
============================================================

Every business, security, workflow and system action must create exactly one immutable Audit Event.

Audit records are append-only.

Never modify or delete audit history.

Every Audit Event automatically links to Timeline, Workflow,
Security, API, Configuration and Compliance records.

Audit is the legal source of truth for operational evidence.
