🔒 KAI DATA & IDENTITY DESIGN STANDARD

This becomes one of the most critical standards because **everything inside KAI revolves around Identity**, not records.

---

# PURPOSE

KAI does not manage records.

KAI manages identities.

Every person,

organization,

project,

requirement,

campaign,

submission,

visa,

deployment,

becomes a permanent organizational identity.

Data changes.

Identity never changes.

---

# IDENTITY PHILOSOPHY

Everything exists only once.

Everything relates.

Nothing duplicates.

Identity survives forever.
