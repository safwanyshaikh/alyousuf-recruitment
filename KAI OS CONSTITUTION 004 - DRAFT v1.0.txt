# KAI OS CONSTITUTION 004

# INFORMATION CONSTITUTION

## The Right Information. At The Right Time. For The Right Decision.

**Status:** DRAFT v1.0

---

# PURPOSE

Information is not intelligence.

More information does not create better recruitment.

Correct information,

presented at the correct moment,

to the correct person,

creates operational speed.

KAI therefore governs information before interface.

No screen shall display information simply because it exists.

Every piece of information must justify its presence.

---

# INFORMATION HIERARCHY

Every screen shall present information in exactly this order.

```text
Decision

↓

Next Action

↓

Priority

↓

Risk

↓

Recommendation

↓

Operational Summary

↓

Evidence

↓

Timeline

↓

Reference Information

↓

Historical Information

↓

Raw Data
```

No module may violate this hierarchy.

---

# ARTICLE I001

## Information Exists Only When Action Exists

If information cannot change an operational decision,

it shall not occupy primary screen space.

---

# ARTICLE I002

## Primary Information

Primary information always answers

What requires my attention now?

Examples

Urgent Requirement

Interview Today

Candidate Waiting

Submission Pending

Offer Expiring

Visa Delay

Mobilization Blocked

---

# ARTICLE I003

## Secondary Information

Secondary information supports the decision.

Examples

Experience

Qualification

Nationality

Current Location

Employer

Trade

Gulf Experience

Certificates

---

# ARTICLE I004

## Reference Information

Reference information remains available

without competing with operational information.

Examples

Passport Number

Email

Address

Previous Salary

Blood Group

Emergency Contact

Old Notes

---

# ARTICLE I005

## Historical Information

History shall never interrupt today's work.

History remains permanently available

through

Timeline

Drawer

Activity History

Audit

Previous Deployments

Communication History

---

# ARTICLE I006

## Information Density

Every screen shall answer the operational question

without overwhelming the recruiter.

More information is not better.

Better organised information is better.

---

# ARTICLE I007

## Progressive Disclosure

KAI reveals information progressively.

Level 1

Immediate Decision

↓

Level 2

Operational Details

↓

Level 3

Evidence

↓

Level 4

History

↓

Level 5

Complete Record

The recruiter decides how deep to explore.

---

# ARTICLE I008

## Operational First

Operational information always appears before master data.

Never display

Address

Gender

Passport

Email

before displaying

Next Action

Risk

Requirement

Priority

---

# ARTICLE I009

## Context Preservation

Information shall always preserve context.

Candidate information shall always remember

Requirement

Campaign

Submission

Recruiter

Client

Project

Conversation

without requiring separate searches.

---

# ARTICLE I010

## Information Never Repeats

The same information shall never appear multiple times on one screen.

One source.

One display.

Many consumers.

---

# ARTICLE I011

## Every Record Has One Truth

Every operational value has one authoritative source.

Examples

Candidate Name

Requirement Quantity

Client Name

Project Name

Submission Status

No duplicate editable values.

---

# ARTICLE I012

## Information Freshness

Operational information must always display

Current

Updated

Waiting

Expired

Outdated

Stale information must become visually distinguishable.

---

# ARTICLE I013

## Time Before Detail

Recruiters think in

Today

Tomorrow

This Week

Overdue

Not timestamps.

Time shall therefore be presented operationally before technically.

---

# ARTICLE I014

## Human Readability

Information shall be written for recruiters,

not databases.

Examples

"Interview Tomorrow"

instead of

"Interview Date : 28/06/2026"

"Offer Awaiting Acceptance"

instead of

"Offer Status = Pending"

Operational language always wins.

---

# ARTICLE I015

## One Scroll Rule

The primary operational decision for every module shall be visible without excessive scrolling.

The recruiter should understand the situation before exploring details.

---

# ARTICLE I016

## Relationship Visibility

Every important record shall immediately expose its relationships.

Candidate

↓

Requirement

↓

Project

↓

Client

↓

Campaign

↓

Submission

↓

Recruiter

No hidden relationships.

---

# ARTICLE I017

## Information Ownership

Each module owns its own information.

Clients own business information.

Projects own commercial information.

Requirements own manpower demand.

Campaigns own sourcing information.

Submission owns client movement.

Mobilization owns travel information.

Visa owns compliance.

Deployment owns employment completion.

No module edits another module's ownership.

---

# ARTICLE I018

## Confidence Visibility

Whenever information is inferred,

calculated,

predicted,

or recommended,

confidence must be visible.

Users must distinguish

Fact

Inference

Recommendation

Prediction

---

# ARTICLE I019

## Noise Elimination

Decorative statistics,

unused counters,

vanity metrics,

duplicate KPIs,

and passive reports

shall never occupy operational space.

Every visible metric must support today's work.

---

# ARTICLE I020

## Information Lifetime

Information moves through three states.

Active

↓

Reference

↓

Archive

Nothing is deleted from organisational intelligence without authorised policy.

---

# INFORMATION VALIDATION

Every future screen must answer

1.

What decision does this information support?

2.

Is this information actionable?

3.

Can it appear later?

4.

Can it appear only on demand?

5.

Is another module already responsible for displaying it?

6.

Will showing this reduce or increase cognitive load?

If uncertainty exists,

the information shall not appear on the primary workspace.

---

# LOCK CONDITION

Information serves decisions.

It never competes with them.

Every interface,

dashboard,

workspace,

drawer,

timeline,

table,

assistant,

and report inside KAI shall comply with the Information Constitution.

The Information Constitution derives from

Human Constitution

↓

Operational Constitution

↓

Decision Constitution

↓

Information Constitution

No future implementation may violate this order.
